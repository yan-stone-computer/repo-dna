# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-06

- 🧬 First scan: commits, contributors, bus factor, language breakdown,
  weekday × hour activity heatmap, night-owl index, weekend-warrior ratio,
  chaos coefficient, hot files, longest streak, and the Helix Score.
- 🖼 Shareable SVG card (`--svg`) designed for embedding in your README.
- 🌐 Self-contained preview page (`--html --open`) with a one-click SVG download.
- 🖥 Local web dashboard (`--web`): point-and-click scanning in your browser —
  heatmap, language bars, hot files, card download. Loopback-only, token-guarded.
- 📤 JSON output (`--json`) for scripts, bots and CI.
- 🔌 Zero dependencies. Nothing ever leaves your machine.
